# TrustKey Locksmith — about us

> For the whole team: managers, technicians, everyone. A 5-minute read.
> Everything here is true about how we already work. If something doesn't match real life, tell the owner.

## Who we are

**TrustKey Locksmith** is a local mobile locksmith based in **Mesa, Arizona**. We opened in **June 2026**. There's no shop and no office for customers: we go to them. We work **24/7**: the regular price during the day (7AM–8PM), +$60 after 8PM.

**What we do:** open cars, homes and businesses; make car keys — from a plain key to a smart key, including when every key is lost; rekey and install locks; open safes; extract broken keys.

**Where we go:** Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Tempe, Scottsdale, Phoenix, Peoria, Queen Creek, Ahwatukee.

**Who does the work:** only our own technicians, never subcontractors. The tech wears a uniform and drives a vehicle with the TrustKey logo.

## Six promises to every customer

| Promise | What it looks like on the job |
|---|---|
| **Price first, pay after** | The customer hears the full price before we drive out. For keys it's the starting price, and the tech gives the exact one before any work starts. They pay when the job is done |
| **The truth, even if it costs us the job** | We're not licensed — we say so. The car is dealer-only — we send them to the dealer. The fob battery is dead — we tell them to change the battery. We never make things up |
| **We protect the customer** | We always check documents: ID, and for a car the registration. Keys and access go only to the owner |
| **Careful work** | We open without damage first. If a lock has to be drilled, we explain and ask first |
| **We keep our word** | What the manager promised, the tech delivers. Arrival time is an honest window. The customer gets a text with the tech's name and photo |
| **We stand behind our work** | A 30-day warranty on keys we make and locks we install. Our own technicians, never subcontractors |

## The price from the phone is our price

When a manager quotes a price, the whole company has made a promise.

1. On site, the tech sees the job is harder than it sounded on the phone. The tech **explains once, calmly**, what is different.
2. If the customer holds to the phone price, the tech apologizes and **does the job for that price**. No arguing, no pressure, no "that's not how it works".
3. Afterwards the tech tells the owner, and **we sort out the difference together**.

> "You're right — that's what you were told on the phone. Sorry for the mix-up — I'll do it for [price]."

For keys, the manager says "from" and that the tech gives the exact price before any work. That is exactly what we do — it's not breaking our word, it's what the customer was promised.

**For managers:** this is why we quote only from the price book, and exactly. A wrong price on the phone costs the company money and puts the tech in an awkward spot.

## Where there's conflict, we're not there

We don't make money on someone else's trouble.

- **We don't evict people.** A landlord asks us to open a tenant's apartment or change the tenant's lock while they're away — we say no. We don't know why the person isn't paying, and in Arizona only a court can evict.
- **We don't get between people.** Another adult in the home objects to the opening — we don't open.
- **A tech may always walk away.** If you feel unsafe or something doesn't add up, decline politely and leave: "I'm sorry, we're not able to help with this one." Then tell the owner. No one gets in trouble for that.

## Rules we never break

| Rule | In short |
|---|---|
| Life before locks | A child or a pet locked in a car, someone unwell, smoke, gas — 911 first, then us |
| Documents | No documents, no opening. Documents inside — we check them right after opening. None at all — we decide with the owner |
| Nothing made up | Never say "licensed", "bonded", "1-year warranty", a guessed "15 minutes", "since 2010". Don't know — "Let me check and call you right back" |
| Discounts | Managers don't give discounts. The $20 off is only for requests sent through the form on our website |
| Competitors | We don't badmouth them. We talk about how we work |
| Installs | Only under $1,000 for the whole job. Panic bars — never: they require a contractor's license |
| Calm | No arguing and no raised voices with a customer. Can't agree — call the owner |

## When you're not sure

Call or text the owner. Better to ask than to promise a customer something we don't do.
